# Quarto human nomenclature

This document is the canonical guide for new human-facing language. It changes no frozen source identity, authority term, state name, report, filename, evidence key, or hash.

## Vocabulary

| Term | Preferred meaning | Kind | Does not mean | Legacy/source wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **QUARTO** | The whole machine and its human-facing project name. Quarto is long, dual-envelope, open-stern, and has four articulated lateral assemblies plus an inspectable propulsion handover. | Human/project identity; not authority | Flight, aircraft, GEV, creature, cockpit, weapons, or propulsion performance | The engineering lineage remains **MT1**. Local material records `Godot P1A R7`, but does not establish P1A as a Quarto/MT1 designation; no `P1A Quarto` branding is asserted. |
| **FOLIO** | One complete articulated lateral assembly. There are four: front port, front starboard, rear port, and rear starboard. Rear folios are larger and higher/aft; forward folios are lower/forward. | Human-facing mechanism term | A profession such as “wing,” or permission to change implementation identity | Frozen source and archives commonly use **book** / `BOOK`. First explanatory use may say “folio (legacy/source: book).” |
| **HAUNCH** | The characteristic canted/folded packing posture and shoulder relationship produced by the frozen ~70° nested geometry; a folio may be “on haunch.” | Posture/geometry language | A component class, mesh family, state variable, or new authority state | Historical studies already use `haunch` for the 70° packing study. Frozen pose/state names remain unchanged. |
| **CHINE** | The long faceted presentation shoulder of accepted BODY-SHELL-03.1, including the relationship of its four C-sockets to the hull. | Presentation language | The frozen ventral keel, a structural right, an authority solid, or a new volume right | BODY-SHELL-03.1 source uses `BODY_CHINE_*`; that source identity stays unchanged. |

| **BODY ON / BODY OFF** | Within BODY-SHELL-03.1, BODY ON shows the accepted presentation shell; BODY OFF exposes the frozen mechanism/authority view. | Presentation/view language | A mechanism state, authority mutation, geometry promotion, or permission for the shell to own space | Existing toggle labels remain valid. BODY OFF is mechanism truth; BODY ON is presentation. |

## Non-negotiable identity rules

- **“Folio” does not authorize renaming frozen `BOOK`/`book` source identities.** This includes mesh/object names, registration IDs, functions, parameters, evidence keys, test fixtures, and archival reports.
- **“Chine” is presentation-shell language, not the ventral keel.** BODY-SHELL-03.1 creates no authority, structure, keepout, reservation, or volume rights.
- **“Haunch” is a posture/geometry word, not a new state variable.** It must not replace SPREAD, DRIVE, STRUCTURAL_READY, or any frozen pose name.
- **“Quarto” does not replace MT1-S5HR3R1 as a frozen engineering identifier.** It changes human orientation, not proof identity.

## Historical language

“Mechanical Truth” is no longer the public/project title. It may remain as the name of the development method, as authority/mechanism-inspection language, and in already-produced archival reports.

New prose uses **Hush Basin** for the Godot sister project/gameplay realization and semantic reference. Archival files and screenshots may retain **District Zero**. Do not mass-edit them. Hush Basin and Quarto inform one another semantically; neither is asserted to be a literal 1:1 geometry export of the other.

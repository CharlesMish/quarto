# MT1-VB1 — scope and epistemics

`MT1-VB1` is an informative **Vehicle Balance Study**. It is **not** MT1-S5 and is not certified mechanism authority.

## Purpose

Ask only:

> Under explicit, deliberately broad mass-distribution assumptions, does the certified S4A arrangement exhibit a gross first-order mass / geometric-planform / thrust-line contradiction?

## Forbidden claims

Not asked, not answered:

* will it fly;
* is it stable or controllable;
* lift, hover, GEV, performance;
* vehicle validated.

No CL, CFD, %MAC, static margin, or thrust magnitude.

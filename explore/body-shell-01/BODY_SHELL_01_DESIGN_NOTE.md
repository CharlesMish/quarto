# MT1-BODY-SHELL-01 — Design Note

Status: **NON-AUTHORITATIVE CONCEPT**  
Source mechanism: **MT1-S5HR3R1**

This first body proposal tries to make the mechanism read as one vehicle by establishing a thin central dorsal/ventral spine, a clear forward terminus, restrained side chines, and a split open fairing around the fixed-core/thrust-can zone. The four lateral mechanisms remain the visual protagonists: the concept stops at their roots and uses small shoulder gestures rather than enclosing their moving volumes.

Deliberately exposed:

- the thrust-can centerline and handover hardware;
- the open upper service slot around propulsion;
- all four lateral mechanisms, hinges, rails, catches, and lock close-ups;
- the central structural frame wherever it explains load and motion.

Still unresolved and explicitly not claimed here:

- cockpit or crew volume;
- intake, engine-internal, or exhaust architecture;
- doors and production sealing around every swept mechanism;
- body structural authority, manufacturing, tolerance, or load substantiation;
- aerodynamic shaping or CFD.

Every `BODY_*_VIS` mesh is `presentationOnly=true`, `physical=false`, family `body-shell-concept`, and is rooted outside the MT1 authority tree. `BODY OFF / BODY ON` is a visibility toggle only.
